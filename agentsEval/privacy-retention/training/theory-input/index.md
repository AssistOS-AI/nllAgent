# Authority input index

The sole normative theory for this build is
[`../../authority/retention-rules.md`](../../authority/retention-rules.md). Read it in full, preserve its controlled
rule identifiers and source distinctions, and copy the accepted source into `generated/theory/sources/` during
assembly. The authority is fictional and bounded to this executable evaluation; it is not legal advice.

The training implementation must preserve these semantic stress cases from the source:

- the inclusive five-year boundary;
- a documented, record-linked exception with authority and review/end date;
- explicit undocumented evidence and incompatible documented/undocumented evidence;
- absence that is final only under closed exception coverage in the same scope;
- conflicting durations for one record and scope;
- unmapped categories represented as ontology/materialization gaps rather than silent success;
- exact duration, exception, and coverage evidence in every assessment trace.
