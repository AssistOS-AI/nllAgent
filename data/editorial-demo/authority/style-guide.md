# Editorial rulebook

## Rule ED-001: weak phrase in narration

Flag the literal phrase “de fapt” when it appears as whole words in a narrative Markdown paragraph. Do not apply the rule to a paragraph that begins with an em dash or en dash and is therefore treated by this profile as a dialogue line. Do not inspect fenced code blocks. The finding is an editorial warning, cites the exact occurrence, and recommends checking whether the phrase adds necessary contrast.

## Rule ED-002: repeated “parcă” in one narrative paragraph

Flag a narrative Markdown paragraph when the whole word “parcă” appears more than two times in that same paragraph. Two occurrences are allowed. Counts do not cross paragraph boundaries. Dialogue lines beginning with an em dash or en dash and fenced code blocks are excluded. The finding cites the first occurrence above the limit and reports all counted spans.
