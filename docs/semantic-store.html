Hidden physical structures

The current store indexes concepts and roles in maps. Future anchor, temporal, identity, and dependency indexes may replace scans. Their only valid effect is performance; logical values, order, evidence, coverage, and provenance must agree.

Four values

Predicates distinguish TRUE, FALSE, UNKNOWN, and CONFLICT. Unknown is missing support; conflict is incompatible admitted support. Negating unknown remains unknown.
