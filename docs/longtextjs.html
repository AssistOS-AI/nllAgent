Anchors and claims

A span is a validated half-open Unicode code-point range over one exact source revision. A claim connects a ground ontology term to epistemic status and anchors. “Proposed” says how an interpretation entered the program; it is not a truth value.

Long documents

ESM imports are the module system. A project may separate source, identities, contexts, sections, timeline, cross-references, coverage, and the root program. There is no parallel manifest.

Ambiguity and absence

Competing readings remain factorized alternatives. An unresolved mention remains unresolved. Missing vocabulary creates an ontology gap. Missing coverage creates a coverage gap. An absent match does not become a negative claim unless the exact domain and scope are closed.
