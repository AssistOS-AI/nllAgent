A field guide to the ideas behind nllAgent

Concepts, scientific foundations, and ethical commitments

This page explains the vocabulary behind nllAgent, the research traditions from which its architecture borrows, why the boundaries are useful, and what those mechanisms do not guarantee. It is an initiation map for readers coming from literature, law, science, AI, or software engineering.

Start with the unusual meaning of CNL. In this project, CNL historically expands to Constraint Natural Language. It is a controlled output family, not a language in which a person must encode the rulebook. CNL/Audit-1 describes what a released theory established about an existing source. CNL/Plan-1 is the idea-specific specification produced after planning circuits applied that theory. Rules and their executable meaning remain in CircuitJS.

The conceptual map

flowchart LR
  REL[Authority + examples → published release]
  INPUT[Existing document or idea] --> LTX[LongTextJS source world]
  REL --> EXEC[Validation or planning CircuitJS]
  LTX --> EXEC --> CNL[CNL/Audit-1 or CNL/Plan-1]
  CNL -. optional plan realization returns as input .-> INPUT

One theory, two directions. Retrospective circuits audit a fixed source. Prospective circuits specify a future source. Optional realization closes the loop only by returning the candidate to the ordinary audit path.

Why this architecture can work

“Works” has a bounded meaning here. The architecture does not prove that all natural-language interpretation is correct. It makes particular claims easier to inspect, test, reproduce, reject, and improve.

MechanismWhy it helpsWhat it does not prove
Source / judgment separationLongTextJS preserves what was observed and where; CircuitJS separately states how released rules transform observations into an audit or plan.That the semantic observation proposed by a model is automatically correct.
Typed observation contractsA circuit declares the shape, status, scope, cardinality, and coverage it needs. Manual publication links those demands to approved producers.That similarly named concepts in two domains have the same meaning.
Candidate / verifier separationA complex or model-assisted operator can propose a witness; a smaller checker controls publication of the property it knows how to verify.Any semantic property outside the verifier contract.
Explicit coverage and abstentionThe runtime distinguishes “not found in a complete declared scope” from “not observed in incomplete evidence” and can stop rather than invent a verdict.That a source declared complete accurately represents the external world.
Frozen releases and replayable artifactsThe rule, model capture, source revision, trace, and verifier result cannot silently drift during a run.That the approved policy itself is fair, lawful, or complete.
Natural microcases and non-regressionSmall positive, negative, ambiguous, exception, and coverage cases localize whether extraction or theory failed.Universal generalization beyond the intended use and represented document distribution.

Research on long-context models reports that retrieval and use of relevant information can degrade when that information lies in the middle of a long input. This supports the decision to build addressable memory and executable dependencies rather than rely only on a larger prompt; it does not establish that nllAgent's own extraction is correct. See Lost in the Middle and the document-level contradiction benchmark ContraDoc.

The epistemic and ethical constitution

These commitments are architectural requirements, not decorative AI principles.

  1. Source integrity. An interpretation must not replace its source. Published observations and findings retain exact anchors or declared external provenance.
  2. Bounded claims. A certificate names the property actually checked. Deterministic code after an uncertain premise does not upgrade that premise to certainty.
  3. Contestability. A reviewer must be able to contest the passage, identity, scope, coverage, rule, exception, witness, or rendering separately and see what depends on it.
  4. Honest abstention. undetermined, review-required, and stopped states are useful results. Missing evidence must not be translated into compliance or non-compliance.
  5. Authority separation. A production document, model output, or isolated review comment cannot become a rule merely by appearing in the input. Authority changes pass through learning and manual publication.
  6. Non-retroactivity. A new release does not rewrite what an older release concluded. Reanalysis is a new run whose differences remain inspectable.
  7. Human responsibility. Legal, editorial, scientific, and organizational policy owners remain responsible for the intended use and authority. A structured explanation can still induce automation bias; the UI must expose limits rather than use fluency as evidence.
  8. Privacy and least privilege. Source text, model access, agent workspaces, and benchmark data receive only the capabilities and retention required by their role. Learning and production stay separate.
  9. Plurality where disagreement is real. Contested rules or interpretations may remain separate candidate worlds or release lineages. Majority or model consensus is not silently converted into truth.

The W3C PROV-O recommendation provides a general vocabulary for provenance. Research on LLM judges demonstrates susceptibility to several evaluation biases, so nllAgent treats model judgments as evidence with a declared ceiling rather than as an anonymous oracle. See Humans or LLMs as the Judge?.

How to read a guarantee

KindResponsible interpretationExample
Mechanically certifiedA checker replayed a precisely stated property from source data and a witness.A quoted phrase occurs at the stated Unicode anchor.
Evidence certifiedEvidence, scope, rubric use, and control flow were checked, but at least one semantic premise remains model-assisted.A candidate passage is grounded in the declared scene and focalizer evidence.
Model judgmentAn evaluated model applied a bounded rubric. The result remains an interpretation, not a theorem.A passage explains an emotion too directly for one editorial policy.
Human confirmedAn authorized reviewer accepted a claim for the specified policy and release. An editor confirms the intended focalization exception.
Stopped or undeterminedThe system lacked a required concept, resource, complete source, budget, or unambiguous interpretation.A missing official register prevents a negative compliance conclusion.

Programs and outputs

ConceptMeaning in this projectImportant distinction
DSLA domain-specific language whose vocabulary and rules are narrower than a general programming language.JS-like syntax does not grant JavaScript capabilities.
LongTextJSThe declarative instance program for one source: structure, anchors, observations, alternatives, coverage, gaps, and task.It represents the source world; it does not own compliance policy.
FoundationPackA versioned platform overlay that contributes bounded observations, invariants, circuits, and limitations to ordinary runs.foundation-core is not a universal ontology and can be disabled for a different world model.
KnowledgePackA proposed optional package for sourced, dated, jurisdiction- and world-scoped contingent facts.The contract boundary is specified, but loading and governance are not implemented.
CircuitJSThe typed declarative graph for reusable theory: observation bindings, transformations, state, exceptions, operators, verification, and emission.It is versioned theory; it is not arbitrary JavaScript or an opaque prompt.
CNL/Audit-1The controlled natural-language audit profile assembled from verified findings about an existing document.It may contain compliance, non-compliance, quality, ambiguity, or insufficient-evidence observations.
CNL/Plan-1The controlled natural-language document specification produced after planning circuits apply released rules to one idea.It is not a natural-language copy of those rules and does not itself prove a later realization compliant.
ASTAn abstract syntax tree: the plain-data structural representation compiled from a restricted author form.The runtime executes the canonical graph, not unrestricted source code.
IRAn intermediate representation used between human-facing source and execution or storage.LongTextJS is deliberately not one universal ontology for all reasoning.
Observation contractThe typed interface declaring exactly what LongTextJS material a circuit consumes and under what coverage and status conditions.Matching names are insufficient; operational meaning and producer alignment are checked.
OperatorA registered transformation or solver with a signature, effects, budget, output contract, and often a checker.A model call is one operator class, not the runtime's universal judge.
WitnessConcrete evidence supplied so a verifier can replay one declared property. A witness is data, not a fluent chain-of-thought narrative.
Verifier / certificateThe checker and its accepted result for a bounded property.A certificate inherits the weakest unverified premise on which its claim depends.
Guarantee ceilingThe strongest guarantee an output may retain after considering all material premises.A deterministic node cannot turn a proposed semantic observation into a mechanical fact.
Verification dominanceThe static property that every dependency path into publication passes exclusively through accepted verification.A candidate-to-emit shortcut is rejected at compilation.
Topological orderA deterministic node order in which every ordinary dependency runs before its consumer.Cycles require an explicit reasoning construct rather than an accidental graph loop.
FindingA structured conclusion with rule, source anchors, verdict, witness, guarantee, remediation, and limits.The Markdown sentence is a rendering of this object, not the authority.

Learning and operational lifecycle

ConceptMeaningWhy it exists
NaturalLanguageLinterProjectThe durable intended use, authority, calibration corpus, review evidence, operational context, and release history for one specialized agent.Prevents “the agent” from being confused with one prompt or run.
ReleaseAn immutable, content-addressed package of schemas, producers, circuits, verifiers, compatibility declarations, and benchmark evidence.Freezes the theory used by a result.
Content addressingIdentifying an immutable artifact by a digest of its canonical content. A matching digest proves byte identity, not semantic correctness.
Semantic version / diffA version and comparison that describe changes in meaning and likely impact, not only changed file lines.A major meaning change creates new release identity rather than rewriting prior runs.
RunOne transactional application of a precise release to one precise input and task. Provides reproducible identity and failure status.
MicrocaseA small natural-language example with separately expected observations and circuit behavior.Localizes extraction, theory, exception, and coverage failures.
BenchmarkVersioned public, development, hidden, long-scenario, metamorphic, and mutation evidence for an intended use.Tests a release without claiming universal correctness.
Manual publicationThe explicit maintainer command that compiles, links, benchmarks, digests, and freezes a candidate before updating the active pointer.Separates the agent proposing a theory from the authority to change production.
Compatibility gateThe run-time proof that this input can satisfy the selected release's contracts.A release can be valid yet inapplicable to an unfamiliar document.
LearningNeed / issueA persistent, typed object explaining a blocked run, disagreement, or missing capability.Turns failure into a reviewable input to a later learning iteration.
RealizationThe optional human or LLM transformation of CNL/Plan-1 into a candidate document.Separates document planning from fluent surface generation.
RevisionA bounded attempt to repair a candidate using the plan and structured audit findings.Does not change the release or allow the generator to certify itself.

Related theories and what nllAgent borrows

Tradition or standardIdea used hereRelationship and limit
W3C Web Annotation, PROV-O, and UIMASeparate an annotation from its target and preserve machine-readable provenance across analysis stages.nllAgent uses its own plain-data source and trace models; it does not claim wire-format compatibility with all three.
JSON Schema and SHACLValidate data shape separately from the data being validated.Shape validity can reject malformed artifacts but cannot prove the intended semantic interpretation.
LegalRuleMLLegal rules require explicit authority, temporal scope, defeasibility, obligations, permissions, prohibitions, and links to source provisions. CircuitJS is not LegalRuleML and legal validity remains tied to an agent's approved authority and intended use.
Event Calculus and Truth Maintenance SystemsEvents can initiate or terminate persistent state; conclusions retain justifications and can be retracted when premises change.These are operator patterns, not a claim that every document uses one temporal logic.
Abstract argumentation frameworks Conflicting arguments and exceptions can remain explicit instead of being flattened into one score. Domain packages must still define how text becomes arguments and what authority governs priority.
Datalog and SouffléTyped relations, fixpoint derivation, indexing, and incremental analysis can make declarative rules executable. CircuitJS also supports non-monotonic state, model calls, search, and external solvers; it is not Datalog.
DPLL(T) and SMT solvingA common control protocol can coordinate specialized theories without pretending that they share one solving algorithm. nllAgent only obtains solver guarantees when a concrete operator and witness checker are installed.
Syntax-Guided Synthesis and CEGISRestrict the program space by a grammar and refine candidates using counterexamples.Learning agents may propose circuits, but manual publication—not agent confidence—controls release.
Proof-Carrying CodeA complex producer can supply a simpler object that a smaller trusted checker validates against a policy.A document witness is not a formal proof unless its verifier contract actually establishes a formal property.
PICARD and grammar-constrained decodingReject model outputs that cannot satisfy a target grammar or schema.Constrained syntax prevents impossible forms, not plausible but semantically wrong observations.
Metamorphic testing and mutation testingTest invariants under controlled input transformations and test whether the suite detects deliberate defects. These techniques measure test sensitivity; they do not replace representative human-confirmed cases.
Neuro-symbolic AIUse language models for ambiguous translation and realization while explicit structures carry persistent state, rules, provenance, and replay.“Neuro-symbolic” names a division of labor, not a blanket guarantee against hallucination.

Similar approaches and the boundary

ApproachOverlapDifference in nllAgent
Classic compiler or linterSource locations, typed intermediate artifacts, reusable rules, diagnostics, and deterministic replay.Natural-language observations can be ambiguous and model-assisted, so status, alternatives, coverage, and review are first-class.
RAGRetrieval can propose source passages relevant to a task.Retrieval rank does not prove complete coverage, stable identity, rule applicability, or a verified conclusion.
One long LLM promptA model can interpret the document and produce useful candidates or prose. The rule release, source world, observation contract, witnesses, and verification exist outside the model call and remain replayable.
Traditional controlled natural languageBoth seek language with more explicit, stable interpretation than unrestricted prose.Users do not author the rulebook in CNL here. nllAgent emits CNL audits and plans after executing CircuitJS.
Policy or rule languagesRules are explicit, versioned, and executable.CircuitJS also orchestrates document observations, model-assisted operators, state, search, verification, and explanations.
Proof assistant or theorem proverSmall trusted checking and proof-producing workflows are architectural inspirations.Most literary or legal interpretations are not formal theorems. Their guarantee must remain evidence-certified, model-judged, or human-confirmed.

Implemented mechanism versus research lineage

The page deliberately includes concepts broader than the current demonstration agent. The repository currently implements canonical LongTextJS, the bounded foundation-core overlay, restricted CircuitJS validation and planning graphs, observation contracts, registered operators and replay verifiers, CNL audit and plan artifacts, compatibility and coverage gates, immutable releases, natural benchmark checks, isolated learning, and optional bounded realization. Event-calculus-style state, argumentation, solver, multimodal, and richer legal modules are extension patterns unless a selected published release actually includes them and provides the required evidence. A scientific citation explains a design lineage; only code, tests, release evidence, and an applicable run establish that a capability exists for a document.
